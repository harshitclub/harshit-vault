# Prompt Engineering

Techniques and structural patterns to steer large language models toward deterministic, accurate, and high-quality outputs.

## Core Patterns

- **Role Prompting**: Assigning persona, expertise, and operational boundaries.
- **Few-Shot Prompting**: Providing representative input-output pairs to establish format and reasoning standards.
- **Chain of Thought (CoT)**: Enforcing step-by-step reasoning before final deduction.
- **ReAct (Reason + Act)**: Interleaving internal reasoning traces with action execution and observation parsing.
